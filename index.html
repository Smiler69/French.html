How does it work — is it an app I download?

It's a single HTML file that works in any web browser — Safari, Chrome, Firefox. Open it on your iPhone, iPad, or computer. No app store, no installation, just open and go.

Will I lose my progress if I close the browser?

Your progress saves automatically in your browser. The app also has a backup feature — download a save file at any time and restore it on any device.

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely. The Daily 10 feature starts with the most essential words and builds gradually. Grammar tips explain the rules as you go, with no prior knowledge assumed.

How long will it take to see results?

Most users report recognising words in conversation within two to three weeks of daily practice. The spaced repetition system is designed to make learning efficient rather than time-consuming.
